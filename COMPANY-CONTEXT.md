## 4. Services

### Four Primary Service Pillars

#### Skin Rejuvenation

Natural-looking skin and aesthetic care:

- Botox / Dysport (neurotoxins)
- Cosmetic Dermal Filler (volume restoration, contouring — Restylane®, Revanesse®, Sculptra®)
- Customized UltraFacial (HydraFacial MD® at Victoria/Langley; AquaFirme® at Kelowna)
- Laser & IPL/BBL Photorejuvenation (ICON® IPL System at Victoria/Langley; Sciton® BBL at Kelowna)
- The Perfect Derma™ Peel (chemical peel with Glutathione)
- Medical Microneedling (collagen induction)
- Dermaplaning (manual exfoliation)
- Belkyra™ (submental fat/double chin reduction)
- PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma)

#### Hormone Balancing

Bio-Identical Hormone Replacement Therapy (BHRT) for men and women at all ages. MD-directed programs including hormone testing, physician consultation, nutritionist consultation, and ongoing follow-up. Restores balance and vitality through personalized hormone protocols.

#### Biohacking / Longevity Medicine

Cellular optimization, performance, recovery, longevity. "Train hard. Recover smarter."

- **All Locations**: PBM/Red Light Therapy, PEMF
- **Langley & Kelowna**: HBOT (Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy), Far Infrared Sauna
- **Kelowna Only**: IV Therapy, Brain Tap
- **Langley Only**: Neurointegrator
- **Victoria & Langley**: NuCalm

#### Healthy Weight Management

Physician-supervised, sustainable weight loss and metabolic health. Root-cause approach addressing gut, metabolism, and hormones. Personalized dietary/supplement protocols. Includes a refund guarantee within 3 months if unsatisfied with results. Nutritional counseling and tiered programs for different financial stages.

- Real photography over AI-generated images
- Longevity/vitality framing: full-body lifestyle shots, natural lighting, movement
- Soft shadows, strong spacing hierarchy, large readable headings
